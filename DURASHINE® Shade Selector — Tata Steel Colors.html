WHY DURASHINE

Built on steel, finished for the eye

Corrosion resistant

An Aluminium-Zinc-Silicon alloy coating gives roofs 2–3× longer life than ordinary colour-coated sheets in similar conditions.

High tensile strength

Higher yield strength than conventional sheets means better load-bearing and greater resistance to wind and impact.

Cooler interiors

Reflective, oven-baked finishes cut down heat radiation into the building, keeping interiors more comfortable year-round.

A colour for every wall

A wide, considered palette across roofing, wall cladding and liner profiles for residential, commercial and industrial builds.
